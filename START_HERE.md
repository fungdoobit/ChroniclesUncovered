# Copy-paste this to Claude Code

I am building a personal history website.

Before doing anything, read these files in full:

- CLAUDE.md
- HISTORY_RESEARCH.md
- SOURCES.md
- SITE_BLUEPRINT.md

They contain the project's editorial rules, the history research already completed, authoritative starting sources, and my intended direction.

I want the website to begin with a strong focus on Sabah / North Borneo, Malaysian and Southeast Asian history, especially overlooked, complicated or dark historical stories. Later I may cover other history I find interesting and adapt articles into videos.

Important:
- Do not treat HISTORY_RESEARCH.md as finished publication copy.
- Preserve the distinction between established fact, interpretation, disputed claims and counterfactual speculation.
- Historical accuracy matters more than making a story dramatic.
- Never invent a source, date, quotation or statistic.
- For controversial history, show credible competing interpretations instead of flattening the story.
- Prefer primary documents, archives, governments, museums, universities and scholarly sources.
- Keep the writing readable and engaging rather than academic.
- Keep Sabah's perspective central when the subject concerns Sabah.

First, inspect the existing repository and tell me what stack/project structure is already present.

Then propose the best implementation plan for turning it into this history site.

Unless the existing stack makes it inappropriate, I want:
- content stored in Markdown or MDX
- reusable article templates
- categories/tags
- article source sections
- support for timelines
- room for maps and interactive stories later
- strong mobile reading experience
- a modern editorial look rather than a stereotypical old-paper history theme

After showing me the plan, begin implementing the foundation of the site using the existing project rather than rebuilding things unnecessarily.

For initial sample content, do NOT fabricate a fully researched article. Use clearly labelled placeholder/sample content or use only claims supported by the supplied research and sources.

The first real article I want to work toward is:

"Malaya Was Not Malaysia: Why 1957 and 1963 Are Different"

After the site foundation is working, help me research and fact-check that article properly before publishing it.
